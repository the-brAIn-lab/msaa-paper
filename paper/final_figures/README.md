# Final figures

The figures as they appear in the paper and supplement, under their manuscript numbers.
`*.eps` are the exact files the manuscript includes; `*.pdf` are the vector renders
they were made from (Figure 2 is converted from its EPS).

Figures 1 and 2 are conceptual schematics and are not generated by this repository.
Every other figure is drawn by `scripts/build_figures.py` (`make figures`); main Figures 5
and 6 combine the bundle panels 4A/4B and 4C/4D into one page. See the figure-number
mapping in the top-level README.
